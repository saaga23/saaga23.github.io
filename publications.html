Bridging the Domain Gap: Transfer Learning and Aggressive Fine-Tuning for Robust Plant Disease Detection in Low-Resource African Agriculture

Sunday Aspita Abraham, Olanloye Dauda Odunayo, Abidemi Emmanuel Adeniyi, Qurrat Ul Ain Mughal — Proceedings of Machine Learning Research 319 (2026)

Published

Field-collected dataset from real farms in southwestern Nigeria. Two-phase progressive fine-tuning of MobileNetV2 pushes tomato accuracy from 34.69% to 97.96% and cassava from 52.42% to 98.39%. Key insight: in low-resource agriculture, training discipline matters more than architecture choice.

Dataset: 1,742 photographs (Ibadan & Osun States) Hardware: Free Kaggle CPU (no GPU)

Graph Spectral Entropy: A Neuro-Symbolic Quality Control Metric for Industrial Generative AI Safety Instruction Verification

Sunday Aspita Abraham — IEEE IES Generative AI Challenge 2026

Selected for challenge track

Proposes Graph Spectral Entropy (GSE), a neuro-symbolic metric operating on the signed Laplacian of sentence dependency-parse graphs to detect safety-critical semantic corruption in LLM-generated industrial text. Hybrid classifier achieves ROC-AUC 0.903 (F1=0.835) on 1,191 OSHA sentence pairs, outperforming BLEU (0.612) and BERTScore (0.622) by ~0.29 AUC points. Produces bootstrap confidence intervals compatible with EU AI Act Article 9.

Dataset: 1,191 sentence pairs (29 CFR Part 1910) Latency: <10 ms per pair

AfriKnow: Calibration Disparity on Culturally Sensitive African versus European Knowledge in LLMs from Two Families

Sunday Aspita Abraham — Under review

Under Review

A 2,520-record calibration audit of seven LLMs (Claude-Sonnet-4.6, DeepSeek-V3.2, Gemini-2.5-Flash-Lite, Gemma-4-31B, GPT-4o-mini, GPT-4.1-Nano, Llama-3.3-70B) using Global-MMLU African vs European splits. Finds that the previously reported "Africa is overconfident" pattern is largely an artifact of benchmark source selection. Deployed end-to-end at ~$0.66 with zero human annotation.

Beyond Hallucination: Parameter-Free Retrieval Outperforms Distilled Transformers in Low-Resource Yoruba Translation

Sunday Aspita Abraham — IndabaX Nigeria 2026, Track 9: NLP & Low-Resource Languages

Rejected

A two-stage BM25 + NCD re-ranking pipeline achieves BLEU 32.40 vs NLLB-200-600M's 8.78 on OPUS-100 Yoruba. Bootstrap resampling (N=1,000) confirms statistical significance (p < 0.05). Demonstrates green AI and hallucination-resistant translation on commodity CPUs.
