Research

My research investigates how AI systems perform in low-resource, African, and safety-critical contexts — and builds practical tools that make them more reliable.

Low-Resource NLP & African Language Technology

I work on NLP for languages with limited digital resources, particularly Yoruba. My work asks whether we can build effective translation and reasoning systems without expensive neural generation — and whether parameter-free methods can outperform distilled transformers on commodity hardware.

Evidence: Beyond Hallucination (IndabaX), ProverbEval-Yoruba (EACL 2027 target), Multi-API Disagreement for African MT (EMNLP 2026 target).

LLM Evaluation & Calibration

I design benchmarks that expose hidden failures in large language models. My AfriKnow audit asks whether LLMs are systematically overconfident about African knowledge — and finds that the popular narrative is mostly an artifact of benchmark design, not model bias.

Evidence: AfriKnow (under review, ARR Oct 2026), ProverbEval-Yoruba.

AI Safety & Industrial Verification

I build neuro-symbolic quality-control metrics for safety-critical AI outputs. My Graph Spectral Entropy metric detects semantic corruption in LLM-generated industrial safety instructions before they reach human operators.

Evidence: Graph Spectral Entropy (IEEE IES Generative AI Challenge 2026, selected for challenge track), mechanistic interpretability demos.

Computer Vision for African Agriculture

I apply transfer learning to plant disease detection using field photographs from Nigerian farms. The central finding: in low-resource agriculture, training discipline (progressive fine-tuning protocols, aggressive regularization) matters more than choosing a larger architecture.

Evidence: Plant Disease Detection (PMLR 319, 2026).

Green AI & Efficient Deployment

I advocate for AI systems that run on commodity CPUs and free-tier hardware. My work on parameter-free retrieval, progressive fine-tuning, and Rust-based systems reflects a commitment to making AI accessible in infrastructure-constrained environments.

Evidence: context-rs (Rust Africa Hackathon), Kaggle CPU-trained plant disease model, BM25+NCD Yoruba translation pipeline.

Cultural AI & Westernized Bias

I investigate whether state-of-the-art LLMs truly understand non-Western knowledge or whether they rely on cultural shortcuts. This includes benchmarking proverbs, calibrating knowledge across cultures, and designing evaluation protocols that surface Westernized bias.

Evidence: ProverbEval-Yoruba, AfriKnow.
