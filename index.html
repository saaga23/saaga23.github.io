Research Summary

I investigate how large language models perform on knowledge and reasoning tasks that reflect real African contexts — and build practical systems that make AI safer, more calibrated, and culturally grounded.

My work sits at the intersection of low-resource NLP, LLM evaluation, AI safety, and applied computer vision. I design benchmarks that expose hidden biases, metrics that verify industrial AI safety, and models that work on commodity hardware in low-resource settings.

Selected Projects

Plant Disease Detection

Smallholder farmers in Nigeria lose up to 80% of crops to undiagnosed diseases.

Built a CNN-based detection system (MobileNetV2) trained on 1,742 manually collected field photographs from Ibadan and Osun States. Delivered as both a web app and an Android mobile app.

97.96% tomato accuracy, 98.39% cassava accuracy — all on free Kaggle CPU hardware. Published at PMLR 2026.

Web App

Graph Spectral Entropy

Industrial LLM-generated safety instructions can silently drop critical constraints (negation, modality, conditions).

Designed GSE, a neuro-symbolic metric operating on signed Laplacians of dependency-parse graphs to detect safety-critical semantic corruption in real time.

Hybrid classifier achieves ROC-AUC 0.903, outperforming BLEU (0.612) and BERTScore (0.622) by ~0.29 AUC. Sub-10ms latency. Selected for IEEE IES Generative AI Challenge 2026.

Code

AfriKnow

Are LLMs systematically overconfident about African knowledge?

Built a 2,520-record calibration audit comparing African vs European knowledge splits across seven LLMs (GPT-4o, DeepSeek, Llama, etc.) using mixed-effects models.

The "Africa is overconfident" pattern is largely an artifact of benchmark source selection. When comparing like-with-like, the calibration gap vanishes. Cost: ~$0.66, zero human annotation.

ProverbEval-Yoruba

Do LLMs understand culturally embedded meaning, or do they rely on Westernized shortcuts?

Constructed a 3,000+ Yoruba proverb benchmark with expert human validation. Benchmarked GPT-4o, Gemini, and DeepSeek under explicit Chain-of-Thought prompting.

Exposes Westernized bias ("Cultural Hallucination") in state-of-the-art models. Targeting EACL 2027.
