Plant Disease Detection

Computer Vision · Agriculture

Problem: Smallholder farmers in Nigeria lose up to 80% of crops to undiagnosed diseases. Existing tools are not trained on African field conditions.

Approach: Built a CNN-based detection system using MobileNetV2 trained on 1,742 manually collected field photographs from Ibadan and Osun States, Nigeria. Two-phase progressive fine-tuning (frozen backbone → unfreeze top 50%). Delivered as a web app (Python/Streamlit) and an Android mobile app (Java).

Result: 97.96% tomato accuracy, 98.39% cassava accuracy — all on free Kaggle CPU hardware. Published at PMLR 2026. Dataset and models publicly available.

Graph Spectral Entropy (GSE)

AI Safety · NLP

Problem: Industrial LLM-generated safety instructions can silently drop critical constraints (negation, modality, conditions), creating liability and safety risks.

Approach: Designed GSE, a neuro-symbolic metric operating on the signed Laplacian of sentence dependency-parse graphs. Detects safety-critical semantic corruption in real time using spectral entropy deltas and a hybrid logistic classifier with 11 structural features.

Result: Hybrid classifier achieves ROC-AUC 0.903 (F1=0.835), outperforming BLEU (0.612) and BERTScore (0.622) by ~0.29 AUC points. Sub-10ms latency per pair. Produces bootstrap confidence intervals compatible with EU AI Act Article 9. Selected for IEEE IES Generative AI Challenge 2026.

AfriKnow

LLM Evaluation · Ethics

Problem: Are LLMs systematically overconfident about African knowledge? The "Africa is overconfident" narrative suggests models are more wrong-but-confident on African topics.

Approach: Built a 2,520-record calibration audit comparing African vs European knowledge splits across seven LLMs (Claude-Sonnet-4.6, DeepSeek-V3.2, Gemini-2.5-Flash-Lite, Gemma-4-31B, GPT-4o-mini, GPT-4.1-Nano, Llama-3.3-70B) sourced from Global-MMLU. Measures verbalized confidence (VCE), Expected Calibration Error (ECE), Brier score, and confidence-hit ratio using mixed-effects models.

Result: The "Africa is overconfident" pattern is largely an artifact of benchmark source selection. Once you compare like-with-like and control for the source of the benchmark, the calibration gap vanishes. Deployed end-to-end at ~$0.66 with zero human annotation.

ProverbEval-Yoruba

Cultural AI · NLP

Problem: Do LLMs understand culturally embedded meaning, or do they rely on Westernized shortcuts when reasoning about non-Western knowledge?

Approach: Constructed a 3,000+ Yoruba proverb benchmark with a Quality Control Layer (expert human validation). Benchmarked GPT-4o, Gemini, and DeepSeek under explicit Chain-of-Thought prompting.

Result: Exposes Westernized bias ("Cultural Hallucination") in state-of-the-art models. Targeting EACL 2027.

Beyond Hallucination: Parameter-Free Yoruba Translation

Low-Resource NLP · Green AI

Problem: Neural machine translation models for low-resource languages like Yoruba require expensive GPU inference and still hallucinate.

Approach: Replaced neural generation with a two-stage parameter-free pipeline: BM25 sparse filtering (k=50, <40ms) + Normalized Compression Distance (NCD) re-ranking using gzip compression.

Result: BLEU 32.40 vs NLLB-200-600M's 8.78 on OPUS-100 Yoruba. Bootstrap resampling (N=1,000) confirms p < 0.05. Stable across sentence lengths. Runs on commodity CPUs.
